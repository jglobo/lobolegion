Flip Stack Privacy Policy

Effective October 20, 2026

Flip Stack does not ask for your name or email, and it has no accounts. The only personal data involved comes from our advertising partner, Google AdMob, and from the app store that handles your purchases.

Flip Stack is a puzzle game made by an independent developer publishing under the name Lobo Legion ("we", "us"). This policy covers the Flip Stack app on iOS and Android.

Information we collect

We do not run our own servers, so we never receive or store your data ourselves.

  • Stored only on your device: your level progress, settings, item counts and whether you bought Remove Ads. This stays on your phone and is never sent to us.
  • Collected by Google AdMob: to show ads, AdMob collects your device's advertising identifier (on iPhone, only if you allow tracking), IP address, approximate location derived from it, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. See the Advertising section below.
  • Handled by the app store: when you make a purchase, Apple or Google processes it. We receive only a confirmation of which item was bought.

Advertising

Flip Stack shows ads through Google AdMob: a banner at the bottom of the screen, a full-screen ad every few levels, and optional video ads you can watch for free items. Google acts as an independent controller of the data it collects.

  • Personalized ads on iPhone: Flip Stack asks for permission to track before any personalized ads are shown. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", you still see ads, but they are not based on your activity in other apps.
  • Users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland: you are asked for consent through Google's consent form before personalized ads are shown.
  • More about Google's practices: read How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

In-app purchases

Purchases such as Remove Ads and item packs are processed entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never see your name, payment card or billing address. Their privacy policies apply to the payment: Apple and Google. The app checks with the store to restore Remove Ads if you reinstall.

Your choices

  • Tracking on iPhone: turn it on or off at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Ad personalization on Android: reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings > Google > Ads (Settings > Privacy > Ads on some phones).
  • No ads at all: buy Remove Ads in the in-game Store to stop banner and full-screen ads. Optional reward videos stay available if you want free items.
  • Delete your data: deleting the app removes everything Flip Stack stored on your device. For data Google collected for ads, use Google's privacy tools.

Children

Flip Stack is made for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child's information was collected through the app, contact us and we will help get it removed.

Data security and retention

Game data stays on your device until you delete the app. Data collected by Google AdMob is kept and protected under Google's own policies. Your information may be processed in the United States and other countries where Google operates.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. Questions or requests about your privacy can be sent to wupinelobo@gmail.com.
