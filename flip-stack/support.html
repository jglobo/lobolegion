Help with the game, purchases and ads

Need help? Email wupinelobo@gmail.com. Tell us your phone model and what happened, and we'll get back to you.

I bought Remove Ads but I still see ads

Open the Store and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account you used to buy it. Optional reward videos for free items stay available after Remove Ads.

I reinstalled the game and lost my progress

Progress is saved only on your phone, so deleting the app also deletes your levels and items. Remove Ads comes back with Restore Purchases. Item packs are used up as you play and can't be restored.

How do I get free items?

During a level, open Items and tap Watch an ad for a free item under Rewind, Pickaxe or Paint Brush. Watch the video to the end to get one free.

A purchase failed or I was charged twice

Payments are handled by Apple or Google. You can request a refund through Apple's Report a Problem page or your Google Play order history. Email us too and we'll help sort it out.
